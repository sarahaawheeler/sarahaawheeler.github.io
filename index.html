Research and applied projects

What should we delegate to AI?

Research

When an AI system can take on a task, does that mean we want it to? I lead a survey study of 366 participants examining how people think about delegating everyday activities and decisions to AI, including questions of trust, autonomy, and oversight.

Human–AI interaction · iTEA Lab · Manuscript under review

Can a conversation help someone change a habit?

Research

In the ACTION Lab, I've studied a motivational interviewing chatbot designed to support healthier choices. I'm interested not just in whether conversational AI can help, but how people respond to it—and what makes that support useful in practice.

Health informatics · Human behavior · ACTION Lab

What can data tell us about financial resilience?

Applied ML

Through Break Through Tech, I'm working with Federal Reserve survey data to model financial vulnerability. Beyond predictive performance, the project considers calibration and interpretability: what can we understand from a model, and what should we be careful not to conclude?

Machine learning · Interpretability · Work in progress
